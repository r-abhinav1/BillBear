How BillBear splits a bill

{{ ui.bear(104, 'Bill Bear') }}

1. The host scans the bill

Bear reads the dishes and prices from a photo. The host fixes anything it got wrong, or types the items in.

2. Everyone joins with a code

Share the six-character code or the QR. No accounts, no app to install.

3. Each person taps what they had

A dish tapped by three people is split three ways. Dishes are marked veg, non-veg, egg or alcohol so they are quick to find.

4. Bear does the maths

Service charge, GST and discounts are added to each share, and the shares add up to the bill total. Download the split as a PDF.
