Manage a guest

Tap Manage next to their name.

{{ ui.icon('nudge', 20) }}

Nudge them

For someone who hasn't sent yet. Sends them a reminder with the room link through your messaging app, or copies it on a computer.

{{ ui.icon('repick', 20) }}

Let them re-pick

Reopens their picks. Their phone asks them to choose again, starting from what they sent. Works the same when splitting by food type.

{{ ui.icon('phone', 20) }}

Move them to a new phone

For a dead battery or a closed tab. You get a one-time move code and a link to send them; they rejoin with the same name and the code, and keep their picks. Without the code nobody else can take their seat.

{{ ui.icon('remove', 20) }}

Remove from the room

Their picks are thrown away and their seat is freed.

People at the table

Change the head count (at least 2), or tap Not coming to drop empty seats so the bill can be split.

{{ mock_stepper("4") }}

Edit dishes

Changing a name, price, quantity, currency or tax and charge row sends everyone back to confirm their picks. Changing veg or non-veg, the tax switch, or the subtotal and bill total printed on the bill, does not. When splitting by food type it's slightly different: moving a dish to another type does ask everyone, and changing only a quantity doesn't.

Split the bill

Possible once everyone has joined and sent their picks. Bear warns you first about dishes nobody picked.

Split by food type

For a big bill: tap Big bill? Split by food type instead under the main button on Check the bill. Type how many had each type for a rate card, or let everyone pick their types. Change your mind from the same link, from the waiting room, or from the rate card. How it works

Fix the bill

Spotted a wrong price, a missing charge or a bill total that doesn't match? Tap Fix the bill on the results page. Everyone's picks stay sent, so after fixing only the total you can split again straight away.

Reopen the picks

Split too early, or someone picked wrong? Reopen from the results page: everyone picks again, starting from what they had.

Save your host link

It gets you back in as host on another phone, or after clearing your browser. Anyone with the link becomes the host, so keep it to yourself.
