Manage a guest

Tap Manage next to their name.

{{ ui.icon('repick', 20) }}

Let them re-pick

Reopens their picks. Their phone asks them to choose again.

{{ ui.icon('phone', 20) }}

Move them to a new phone

For a dead battery or a closed tab. They rejoin with the same name and keep their picks.

{{ ui.icon('remove', 20) }}

Remove from the room

Their picks are thrown away and their seat is freed.

People at the table

Change the head count, or tap Not coming to drop empty seats so the bill can be split.

{{ mock_stepper("4") }}

Edit dishes

Changing a name, price, quantity, currency or tax and charge row sends everyone back to confirm their picks. Changing veg or non-veg, the tax switch, or the subtotal and bill total printed on the bill, does not.

Split the bill

Possible once everyone has joined and sent their picks. Bear warns you first about dishes nobody picked.

Fix the bill

Spotted a wrong price, a missing charge or a bill total that doesn't match? Tap Fix the bill on the results page. Everyone's picks stay sent, so after fixing only the total you can split again straight away.

Reopen the picks

Split too early, or someone picked wrong? Reopen from the results page: everyone picks again, starting from what they had.

Save your host link

It gets you back in as host on another phone, or after clearing your browser. Anyone with the link becomes the host, so keep it to yourself.
