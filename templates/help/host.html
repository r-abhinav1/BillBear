For a dead battery or a closed tab. They rejoin with the same name and keep their picks.

Remove from the room

Their picks are thrown away and their seat is freed.

People at the table

Change the head count, or tap Not coming to drop empty seats so the bill can be split.

{{ mock_stepper("4") }}

Edit dishes

Changing a name, price or quantity sends everyone back to confirm their picks. Changing veg or non-veg, or the tax switch, does not.

Split the bill

Possible once everyone has joined and sent their picks. Bear warns you first about dishes nobody picked.

Reopen the picks

Split too early, or someone picked wrong? Reopen from the results page and split again.

Save your host link

It gets you back in as host on another phone, or after clearing your browser. Anyone with the link becomes the host, so keep it to yourself.
