What happens to the photo

It is sent to an AI model that reads the dishes, prices, quantities and totals, and marks each dish veg, non-veg, egg or alcohol. BillBear does not keep the photo.

Where it slips

  • Blurry, folded or badly lit bills.
  • Quantities. "Idly (2 pcs)" is one plate, not two.
  • Dishes it can't classify are left unmarked.

What you can do

  • Fix anything on the check screen. It tells you when the dishes don't add up to the subtotal.
  • Type the dishes in yourself. Scans are limited each day.
  • Save the scan to a file and open it later without scanning again.

For a clean read

  • Lay the bill flat and fit all of it in the frame.
  • Avoid shadows and glare across the prices.
  • A long bill is fine. One photo of the whole thing beats several pieces.
