What Bear won't allow

  • Claiming more than the bill lists. If all three are claimed, a fourth claim is refused and you're told who has them.
  • Taking the last one from the sharers. While anyone is sharing, one stays unclaimed for them.
  • Sharing when nothing is left. If every one is claimed, there is nothing to split.

Worth knowing

  • Sharing alone? You pay for everything unclaimed until someone else shares too.
  • Two of three claimed and nobody sharing? The third is charged to no one. The host is warned before splitting.
  • Two people send at the same moment? The first one in gets it. The other is told what is left.
  • Changing your picks? What you sent stays on hold for you until you send again.
  • Only one of a dish on the bill? There is nothing to claim. Everyone who taps it splits it equally.
